## 3. Full Description

Experience the timeless wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita in a sanctuary of pure focus. **Scroll the Gita** is designed for genuine contemplation—free from distractions, commercial algorithms, and data collection.

### ✦ 100% Offline & Private
Read anywhere: on airplanes, during silent retreats, or in remote sanctuaries. The entire Bhagavad Gita—including all 700 Sanskrit shlokas, English transliterations, word-by-word meanings, translations, and authentic Sanskrit recitations—is bundled directly on your device. The app requires zero internet permissions and never connects to the outside world.

### ✦ No Ads. No Tracking. No Subscriptions.
There are no advertisements, analytics SDKs, user accounts, push notifications servers, or in-app purchases. Your reading journey, saved verses, and reflections remain strictly private on your device.

### ✦ Mindful Reflection Pauses
Unlike modern social media feeds engineered for continuous scrolling, Scroll the Gita gently invites you to pause after significant verses. Take a quiet breath and allow the sacred wisdom to settle in your heart before moving to the next shloka.

### ✦ Offline Sanskrit Recitations
Listen to clear, authentic Sanskrit audio recitation for each shloka with a single tap. Playback respects audio focus and automatically pauses when you move to the next verse. Prefer reading in silence? Audio controls can be completely hidden in Settings.

### ✦ Curated Reading Journeys
Explore thematic sequences tailored to life’s deep questions and moments of reflection:
- **Gita on Duty (Svadharma)**: Navigating responsibility with integrity and purpose.
- **Gita on Fear and Anxiety**: Finding refuge in eternal stillness and surrender.
- **Gita on Equanimity (Samatvam)**: Cultivating peace amidst success and defeat.
- **Chapter 2 in 7 Days**: A meditative week through the philosophical cornerstone of the Gita.
- **Essence in 18 Shlokas**: One transformative verse from each chapter.

### ✦ Instant Offline Search
Search all 700 verses instantly. Our local full-text search supports Sanskrit Devanagari, diacritic-insensitive IAST transliteration (e.g. searching "karma" matches "karma" and "karmaṇye"), and English translations.

### ✦ Sacred Typography & Themes
- **Midnight Dark**: Soothing deep navy and gold palette (`#101426`) for nighttime contemplation.
- **Warm Parchment**: Organic sand and ink tones (`#F5EBDD`) for daytime reading.
- **Contrast Certified**: Every combination rigorously adheres to WCAG AA/AAA standards (contrast ratios > 12:1).
- **Customizable Sizing**: Adjust text sizing up to 130% for effortless legibility.

### ✦ Ambient Backgrounds with Motion-Safe Fallbacks
Subtle, hand-crafted Compose Canvas backgrounds accompany your reading:
- **Slow Drifting Gradient**: Harmonic horizon aura.
- **Particle Dust**: Faint cosmic dust motes.
- **Mandala Rotation**: Micro-rotating sacred geometry.
- **Parchment Grain**: Organic palm-leaf manuscript texture.
- **Sacred Lotus Bloom**: Symmetrical breathing lotus.
- **Still Motion Guarantee**: Choosing "Still" or enabling Android’s system "Reduce Motion" immediately stops all background animations, preserving 100% stillness and zero frame churn.

### ✦ Shareable Shloka Cards
Share timeless verses with friends and loved ones as beautifully rendered image cards (portrait or square) with elegant Sanskrit typography and wordmark—generated completely offline without third-party servers.
